Is the AT10 right for an intermediate player?

It can be, but be aware it rewards swing speed. If you're solid intermediate (consistent volleys, comfortable bandejas), you'll love the pop. If you're early intermediate, look at the NOX Equation Advanced 3 — easier to control while you build technique. We're happy to recommend by WhatsApp.

What grip size should I order?

For most adult players in India, L2 (Standard) is the right call. Players with smaller hands or those who layer two overgrips should pick L1. Players who play with no overgrip and have large hands → L3. When in doubt, go smaller — you can always add an overgrip to thicken.

How long will this racket last?

With normal recreational play (2-3 sessions/week), expect 12-18 months before performance noticeably drops. Heavy tournament use cuts that to 6-9 months. Apply a frame protector from day 1 and replace the overgrip every 8-12 sessions — both extend life significantly.

Do you ship the original NOX box and warranty?

Always. Every racket ships in sealed original NOX packaging with the manufacturer warranty card. We're an authorized NOX distributor for India — your warranty is honored both with us and NOX Spain directly.

Can I return if it's not the right fit?

Yes — 14-day return window from delivery, as long as the racket is unused and in original packaging. Once you've played with it, we can't take it back (regulatory), but reach out anyway — we'll help you trade up.

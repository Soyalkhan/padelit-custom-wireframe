A note from us

How to choose the right padel racket.

Padel racquets fall into three shapes — round for control, diamond for power, teardrop for balance. Beginners benefit from round frames with even balance and a forgiving sweet spot. Intermediate players often graduate to teardrop, then onto diamond as their swing matures. The weight matters as much as the shape: 360-370g suits most adult players, lighter for control players, heavier for finishers. Still unsure?

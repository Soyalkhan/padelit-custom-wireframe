Brand Story · Est. 2015

Two brothers.
One mission.

NOX was founded in 2015 in Madrid by Mikel and Jose Luis Sánchez — two padel-obsessed brothers who couldn't find a racket that matched their playing style. So they built one.

By 2018, NOX had signed Agustín Tapia. By 2022, they were the official WPT supplier. Today, NOX racquets are played in 38+ countries and trusted by players from beginner courts to world rankings.

"We don't sell racquets. We sell weapons of expression on the court." — Mikel Sánchez, Co-founder

Padelit is the official Indian distributor for NOX. Every racket is shipped direct from Madrid, serial-verified, and tested on Indian courts before it reaches you.
